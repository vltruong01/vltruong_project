# Experience

Truong has experience building FastAPI-based chatbot services and deploying lightweight Python applications.

He focuses on practical engineering constraints: low RAM, low CPU usage, simple deployment, and low operating cost.

This project is designed to deploy on Fly.io with auto-start machines, zero minimum running machines, and CPU-only inference.
